# Support Escalation Process

## Objective

Create a structured escalation workflow for unresolved or high-priority customer issues.

---

# Escalation Workflow

1. Customer issue is logged
2. Initial support agent investigates
3. Issue remains unresolved within SLA timeline
4. Case is escalated to senior support
5. Technical or management review begins
6. Resolution plan is communicated
7. Customer receives status updates
8. Case resolution is confirmed
9. Case is closed and documented

---

# Escalation Levels

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Level 1 | Initial customer support |
| Level 2 | Senior support review |
| Level 3 | Technical specialist involvement |
| Level 4 | Management escalation |

---

# CRM Components

- Case status tracking
- Priority fields
- Escalation alerts
- SLA monitoring
- Activity history
- Resolution notes

---

# Expected Outcomes

- Faster issue resolution
- Better accountability
- Clear escalation ownership
- Improved customer communication
