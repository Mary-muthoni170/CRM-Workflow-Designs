# Support SLA Tracking

## Objective

Track support response and resolution timelines to improve customer service consistency.

---

# SLA Categories

| Priority | First Response | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| High | 1 hour | 24 hours |
| Medium | 4 hours | 72 hours |
| Low | 24 hours | 5 business days |

---

# CRM Tracking Components

- Case created date
- Response timestamps
- Resolution timestamps
- Escalation indicators
- SLA status fields

---

# Dashboard Metrics

- Open cases
- Overdue cases
- Average response time
- Resolution rate
- Escalation percentage

---

# Operational Benefits

- Better customer visibility
- Improved response consistency
- Reduced unresolved cases
- Performance accountability
