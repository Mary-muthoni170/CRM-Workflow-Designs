# Client Onboarding Flow

## Objective

Design a structured onboarding process that improves customer transition, communication, and setup visibility.

---

# Onboarding Workflow

1. Client agreement confirmed
2. Welcome communication sent
3. Client information collected
4. CRM profile created
5. Internal onboarding tasks assigned
6. Product or service setup initiated
7. Training or orientation completed
8. Progress follow-up conducted
9. Client onboarding completed

---

# CRM Components

| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Store client records |
| Tasks | Track onboarding activities |
| Notes | Store onboarding updates |
| Dashboards | Monitor onboarding progress |

---

# Automation Opportunities

- Welcome email automation
- Task assignment automation
- Reminder notifications
- Progress tracking updates

---

# Expected Outcomes

- Better onboarding visibility
- Reduced onboarding delays
- Improved customer experience
- Consistent onboarding process
